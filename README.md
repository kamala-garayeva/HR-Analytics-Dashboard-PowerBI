## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the most costly challenges organizations face. This project analyzes an HR dataset to uncover **why employees leave**, **how compensation and satisfaction interact**, and **which departments or roles are most at risk** — presented through a single, interactive Power BI dashboard that business stakeholders can explore without touching raw data.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

- What is the overall attrition rate, and how does it vary by department, gender, and age group?
- Which factors (salary, satisfaction, work-life balance, tenure) are most associated with attrition?
- How is compensation distributed across job roles, departments, and education levels?
- Is there a noticeable gender pay gap across roles or levels?
- How long do employees typically stay before leaving, being promoted, or changing roles?

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

`HR_Analytics.csv` contains anonymized employee-level records, including:

| Category | Example Fields |
|---|---|
| **Demographics** | Age, Gender, Marital Status, Education |
| **Job Information** | Department, Job Role, Job Level, Business Travel |
| **Compensation** | Monthly Income, Salary Slab, Salary Hike % |
| **Satisfaction & Engagement** | Job Satisfaction, Environment Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance |
| **Tenure & Growth** | Years at Company, Years Since Promotion, Total Experience |
| **Outcome** | Attrition (Yes/No) |

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** — data modeling, DAX measures, dashboard design
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — custom KPIs (attrition rate, average tenure, satisfaction scores)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

- **Attrition Overview** — KPI cards and breakdowns by department, gender, age group
- **Compensation Analysis** — salary distribution across roles and departments
- **Satisfaction Deep-Dive** — relationship between satisfaction, work-life balance, and attrition
- **Tenure & Promotion Trends** — career progression and manager stability over time
- **Interactive Filters** — dynamic slicers for self-service exploration
